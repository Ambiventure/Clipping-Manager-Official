CLIPPINGS MANAGER
Northern Railway PR - daily newspad
===============================================================================

TO RUN IT
    Double-click  "Clippings Manager.exe"  in this folder.

    Nothing needs installing. There is no Python to set up, and the morning's
    work needs no internet: every document is read on this machine and nothing
    is ever uploaded.

    Two things do go online, and only when you ask: "Check for updates", and
    capturing a link (below), which opens that page in Chrome to photograph it.

    Keep this whole folder together. The .exe on its own will not run; it needs
    the "_internal" folder beside it. To put it somewhere convenient, copy the
    whole folder, then right-click the .exe and choose "Pin to taskbar" or
    "Send to > Desktop (create shortcut)".


A MORNING, START TO FINISH
    1. COVER PAGE - choose your standard cover artwork once for each newspad. It
       is remembered on this computer. Set the date; the clipping count fills
       itself in.

    2. ADD TODAY'S CLIPPINGS
       - "From Word" / "From PDF" for the division documents. Either format
         works and gives identical results. The division is read from the file
         name, so send them in as they arrive.
       - "Add photos" for loose images already saved on the machine.
       - Drag images straight from WhatsApp Web onto the dashed panel.
       - Or copy an image anywhere and press Ctrl+V.
       - "+ From links" for digital coverage: paste one link, or a whole
         WhatsApp message with a numbered list of them, tick the ones you
         want, and each story is captured as a clipping. Needs Chrome on the
         PC. For a post on X or Facebook that needs a sign-in, press "Take
         from my Chrome": the link opens in your own Chrome, signed in as you
         are, and the picture is taken from the screen when you press Take
         it. Or press "Sign in for captures" once to have such posts captured
         in the background from then on.
       - Or press "Collect from WhatsApp" at the top and stay in WhatsApp Web:
         right-click a photo and choose Copy image, then select its caption
         and press Ctrl+C. Each photo becomes a clipping, named from its
         caption. Press the button again to stop.

       Most clippings arrive already named: the newspaper, edition and page are
       read out of the document. Delhi and Lucknow print their newspaper name
       into the picture itself, so those still need naming by hand.

       A card with Hindi or Punjabi in its title, newspaper or city shows an
       "English" button at the end of its row; "Hindi to English" above the
       list does every such card at once and lists what it changed. A
       newspaper and city typed into the title box move into their own fields
       in English. A paper the list does not know is spelt out and flagged
       amber for a check. Ctrl+Z puts the Hindi back.

    3. REVIEW - the list is the running order, and what you see is what gets
       exported.
       - Click a clipping to open it full size, where it can be renamed,
         rotated, trimmed, split, excluded or deleted. "Trim…" lets you drag
         the edges in; Ctrl+Z puts them back.
       - Type in the headline box; Enter saves it and moves to the next one, so
         a whole division can be named without touching the mouse.
       - Drag the handle to reorder. An orange line shows where it will land.
       - Tick several to move, merge, leave out or delete them together, or
         use "Move to:" to put them in another file's group.
       - Ctrl+Z undoes anything, including a misdrag across the whole list.

    4. EXPORT - "Generate PDF" or "Word (.docx)". One clipping per page, in the
       order shown, behind a cover carrying the count and the date.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
    Run the built-in check. Open a Command Prompt in this folder and type:

        "Clippings Manager.exe" --selftest

    It tests the parts most likely to be missing from an installation and
    writes a report to your Temp folder. Send that report on if you need help.


WHERE YOUR SETTINGS LIVE
    %APPDATA%\ClippingsManager

    That is each newspad's cover pages and headline style, your export
    preferences, and the newspaper list as it grows. Updating the program never
    touches them.


HINDI NEWSPAPER NAMES
    Devanagari names print correctly on any Windows PC, because the font that
    sets them travels inside this application rather than being looked for on
    the machine. A report built here and a report built on another PC set a
    Hindi masthead identically. Nothing needs installing for it.
